# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-09-29

- Initial public release.
- Adds TEST ONE, DELETE ALL, and STOP controls to Google Photos Albums.
- Automatically handles optional Google Photos deletion confirmations.
- Skips albums without a Delete album action.
- Avoids clicking Leave album.
- Includes local-only status and counters.
- Tested successfully against the Google Photos web UI with a 275-album cleanup.
